About the seriesEach poster is a single field of blended colour — no shapes, no drawing, just four soft bodies of colour printed one over another on a warm-white cotton stock. The numbers refer to the order of the passes, not to editions.

PrintingRiso, two to four spot inks, 300 gsm cotton, 500 × 700 mm, edition of 40, numbered on the reverse. Mounted unframed on 3 mm dibond with a natural fibre hanger.
