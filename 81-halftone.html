Rosette is stable. Push the pitch below 6 px and the screens will beat against each other — that beat is the moiré, and it is the one artefact the press cannot hide.

The tonal build is painted light-to-dark: paper, then eight screens, each rotated a few degrees from the last. Nothing here is a photograph. The face is a stack of measured shapes, screened on a rosette, and lit by whatever the mouse is pointing at.
