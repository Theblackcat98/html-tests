MEMPHIS

GROUP

Ten designers, one rule: nothing may be tasteful. Sottsass, Scala, Lacher, De Pas, D'Urbino and the rest built furniture like it was a poster for itself — laminates fighting laminates, bolts left proud, colour used as structure rather than finish. Then the world bought it, and taste never recovered.
