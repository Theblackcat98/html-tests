Field notes

South-west quarter of the basin, drained by Hollow Creek which leaves the sheet at the southern edge. The ground rises steadily to the north-east through a bench of scree and closes on a single summit; there is no second high point on this sheet.

Every fifth line is an index contour and is labelled in metres. Intermediate lines are drawn at one fifth of that interval. Move the pointer up the sheet to raise the section, or scroll.
