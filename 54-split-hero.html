I build systems, not campaigns. A magazine that runs for eleven years needs a grid that can absorb a photo essay, a results page and a legal notice without redesigning itself. That is the work I am interested in.

Everything is designed at the pace of print and reviewed at the pace of software. I would rather ship a system your intern can operate in year four than a launch film nobody can extend.

Nora Halvorsen, Copenhagen & Lisbon
