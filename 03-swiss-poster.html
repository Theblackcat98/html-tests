Sixty-one posters, twelve journals and one very long argument about the grid, assembled from the museum flat file.

Curator

Dr. Annika Roth, head of the graphic design collection. Working with the estates of Josef Müller-Brockmann and Karl Gerstner.

Eight essentials

  • 01  Akzidenz-Grotesk and Univers, 1957
  • 02  A modular grid, drawn before setting type
  • 03  Photography as information, not decoration
  • 04  One accent colour, used sparingly
  • 05  Flattened perspective, no illusion of depth
  • 06  Journals trimmed flush, square format
  • 07  Type set flush left, ragged right
  • 08  Wholesale as a form of persuasion
