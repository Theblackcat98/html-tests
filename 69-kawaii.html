Small things, cut out carefully

Eight stickers, hand-trimmed, for the flat side of a card or the inside cover of a notebook. Fluorescent pink, mint, butter and sky, printed in four passes on 300 gsm uncoated stock. The white line is the trim allowance — keep your blade outside it.

How to use: drag a sticker anywhere on the sheet; press Enter on a focused sticker to nudge it. Die-cut line 4 mm, trim allowance 3 mm. Ink: fluorescent pink, mint, butter, sky. No varnish, no lamination.
