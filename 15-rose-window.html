Hover a light to read its glass

Fitting 04  /  4.8 m oculus

Rosa
Lucis

Twenty-four lanterns for the outer ambulatory, twelve for the saints, and one for the sun. The glazier worked from the inside of the scaffold and painted reverse grisaille on the back of every pane so the light would read as flesh, not glass.
