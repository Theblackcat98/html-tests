01 — The room
11

Purchases a week

The list is short enough that Matteo can name the boat, the hour and the price for almost everything on your plate.

We buy at the Molo Audace at 06:00, from four boats we have used for nine years. If a skipper cannot tell us where the fish was, we do not buy the fish. Last March we sent back two hundred kilos of red mullet because the answer was a shrug.

Everything else grows within ninety kilometres of the room: San Daniele, the flat-leaf parsley from Muggia, the last of the white radicchio that comes into the market in February. The salt on the bread is from the pans at Sečovlje, eight kilometres from the front door, harvested by a family we have bought from since the second week of opening.

“A plate is a decision about what to leave out. The fire is how you find out whether you were right.” Matteo Albanese — chef, owner, and the only person who touches the fire
Sourced

Red mullet

Line-caught, 4–6 kg, off the Sistiana bank. Bought whole, filleted to order, never more than a day before service.

Grown

Castelvetrano

Two hectares above Muggia on karst rock, dry-farmed. Picked at 05:00 the morning of service because it does not travel.

Harvested

Salt & kelp

Hand-cut pans, Sečovlje. And bladderwrack dried on the roof, which goes into the broth and, ground, into the crust of everything deep fried.

Made here

Olive oil

Pressed in November from Debina olives, kept in tin. Fourteen days of harvest age before it is allowed near a plate.

03 — The kitchen

Matteo
Albanese

Trained in Bologna, cooked in Copenhagen, opened his own fire in Trieste in 2016 because he could not find salt with a story attached to it.

He cooks alone at the pass from 16:00 with one commis, Luca, who has been here longer than anyone except the salt. The brigade is four. Nobody works a double service, and nobody is on the floor while the fire is lit, because Matteo does not trust a flame he did not lay himself.

Before Trieste: Osteria Francescana in Modena, six years, pastry section; Noma in Copenhagen, research. He still writes every menu longhand on a squared pad and throws it away after service.

05 — Getting here

A salt warehouse
on the Riva del Sale

The building is 1908: a bonded store for the pans at Sečovlje, then a boat workshop, then eleven years of nothing. The brick vault is original. The fire is in the middle of the room, which means the room is warmer than a dining room should be and better for it.

Six steps from the pavement. The nearest doorbell is the one marked SALA — not the one marked OFFICE, which is a different family and has nothing to do with us.
