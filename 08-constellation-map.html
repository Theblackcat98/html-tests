The Northern
PlateCirca Polaris

An azimuthal equidistant projection centred on the north celestial pole. The pole sits in the middle; right ascension turns anticlockwise around it and declination falls away as concentric circles. Everything within 34° of the pole never sets — the whole plate is a country of permanent night.
