On the collection

I have always imagined that Paradise will be a kind of library.
Jorge Luis Borges

The light in this room comes from a single banker's lamp and a stub of candle, which means everything above the desk is guesswork. The shelves are uneven because a real library is never arranged by height — it is arranged by who carried the book up last, and how tired they were.

Every spine below is drawn from a working list. Reach into the case and one of them will come away in your hand: click any title in the catalogue and its copy on the shelf will light up instead.
