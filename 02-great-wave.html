神奈川沖浪裏

The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Hokusai watched the swell from a fishing boat off the village of Kanagawa and cut it into wood. The blue is bero-ai — Prussian blue, imported, and dearer than gold.

北斎画印
Print
Kanagawa-oki nami ura, from the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji. Katsushika Hokusai, c. 1831 (Tenpō 2).
Technique
Colour woodblock: keyblock carved in relief, two colour blocks, bokashi gradation for the sky and the hollow of the wave.
Provenance
Museum accession MG.1954.312. Longest surviving impression; a fine vertical grain from the keyblock, as seen in the sky.
On this plate
Vector reconstruction. Foam claws are stroked paths, spray is generated along the crest, parallax is driven by the pointer.
