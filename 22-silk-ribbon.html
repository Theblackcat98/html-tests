Collection 07 — Soie Sauvage

Ribbonin motion

Each length is dyed in the vat, then finished by hand on the frame so the light runs the way the weaver fell the thread. Nothing here is printed. The colour you see is the colour of the dye, and it changes with the hour you open the parcel.
