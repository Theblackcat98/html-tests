Twelve pages. None of them are about anything. This is a complete work, printed at 78 % on the machine at the laundrette, which means the type is mostly right.

We went looking for a subject for eleven months. There was a subject. It was not interesting enough to print, so we printed the hole it left.

Copy freely. Copy weirdly. Do not copy at all — that is also fine and honestly our favourite outcome.
