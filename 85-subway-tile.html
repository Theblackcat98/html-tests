Drawn from the Uttoxeter station. The compass motif came off a 1903 platform dado we pulled during the signalling works; the trellis is the fence panel from the booking hall. Everything is drawn on a 200 mm square so the four tiles can be rotated freely around the centre without breaking the joint.

Drawing notesScale 1 : 1

Compass

Four quarter fans struck from the tile corners, meeting on a 22 mm centre square. The fan is the platform's original hand-drawn terminal mark, redrawn as a ruled arc.

Ferrule

A rotated square with a punched centre and four pin dots on the diagonal. It is the field tile: everything else is set out against it.

Trellis

Four clipped circles on a 100 mm pitch with a three-leaf sprig in each quadrant. The curve breaks at the joint, so grout width matters.

Cove boss

A twelve-ray rosette over two rings — the shelf-back tile. Rotate the repeat a quarter turn and this one lands on the diagonal.
