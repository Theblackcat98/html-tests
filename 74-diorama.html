Yatai,

Twelve centimetres of a noodle cart, built in glass and photographed with the aperture wide open. There are five figures in there: the cook, two regulars, a late couple and a dog that belongs to nobody. It is raining outside the jar. It has been raining since the edition was cast.

Notes from the bench

The noren is five panels because every cart I have eaten at had five. The bulb chain at the front is out of focus on purpose: it is the closest thing to the glass and it is what tells your eye how small everything else is.
