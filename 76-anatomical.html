Bench notes

Lanterna abyssi was drawn from a single specimen brought up in the sounding cup of the Isabella and still burning when it reached the deck. The engraver has been trusted with the surface striations and not with the light, which cannot be cut.

The photophore series runs from behind the lens to the anal fringe in a single line, and does not branch. Vogelsang noted this as the character by which the genus is separated from Photocorynus, whose series always doubles before the tail.
